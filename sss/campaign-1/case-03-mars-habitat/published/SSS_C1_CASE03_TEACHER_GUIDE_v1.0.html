Teacher quick start

Students use game-provided spectral transmission values to reject low total light and explain a wavelength-specific chlorophyll failure.

Correct diagnosis

The light delivery system is filtering out red wavelengths needed for chlorophyll biosynthesis.

Critical values

280 PPFD; 12 m pipe; transmission 92%, 88%, 31%, 12%.

Central distinction

Photon quantity versus spectral distribution.

Collect

Tasks 2-7 and Exit Ticket.

Formal lesson plan

Objectives

  • Interpret PPFD as quantity, not full spectral quality.
  • Analyze exact wavelength-band transmission values.
  • Connect new-growth bleaching to new chlorophyll formation.
  • Reject alternatives and write CER.

Standards

NGSS MS-LS1-6; MS-PS4-2; analyzing and interpreting data; constructing explanations; cause and effect; systems and models.

Materials and preparation

Game or fallback, role packet, pencil, calculator optional. Confirm Case 03 navigation and print at 100%.

Success criteria

  • Exact runtime values used without invented points.
  • Explicit quantity/spectrum distinction.
  • Correct diagnosis and symptom mechanism.
  • No “more brightness always fixes it” language.

Case analysis

Story problem

Older lower leaves retain some green; upper canopy and all new growth is pale yellow to white; roots appear healthy; iron and nitrogen additions did not help.

Evidence channels

Crew

Top-down progression; nutrients failed.

Sensors

280 PPFD; light pipes plus LEDs.

Plants

Healthy roots; new tissue bleaches.

Logs

Quantity does not reveal distribution; red-dependent case mechanism.

References and source-status guidance

  1. Authoritative standard/definition: Illuminating Engineering Society. Photosynthetic photon flux density (PPFD): photons per unit area and time in the 400-700 nm band. Used only to define the quantity metric.
  2. Primary research: McCree, K. J. (1971/1972). The action spectrum, absorptance and quantum yield of photosynthesis in crop plants. Agricultural Meteorology 9:191-216. Measurements across 22 crop species showed wavelength-dependent photosynthetic response with broad blue and red maxima.
  3. Primary research: Hogewoning, S. W., et al. (2010). Blue light dose-responses of leaf photosynthesis, morphology, and chemical composition of cucumber. Journal of Experimental Botany 61:3107-3117. Demonstrates that equal irradiance with different blue:red composition can produce different plant function.
  4. Primary research: Heyes, D. J., Ruban, A. V., and Hunter, C. N. (2003). Protochlorophyllide oxidoreductase: dark reactions of a light-driven enzyme. Biochemistry 42:523-528. Establishes POR as a light-driven chlorophyll-biosynthesis enzyme.
  5. Primary research: Armstrong, G. A., Runge, S., Frick, G., Sperling, U., and Apel, K. (1995). Identification of NADPH:protochlorophyllide oxidoreductases A and B in Arabidopsis thaliana. Plant Physiology 108:1505-1517. Establishes light-dependent protochlorophyllide reduction in angiosperm chlorophyll biosynthesis.
  6. Primary/technical space-horticulture source: Bula, R. J., et al. (1994). Light emitting diodes as a plant lighting source. NASA Technical Reports Server. Controlled-environment lighting must provide adequate PAR flux and spectral regions needed for normal development.
  7. Primary/technical space-horticulture source: Bula, R. J., et al. (1991). Light-emitting diodes as a radiation source for plants. HortScience/NASA record. Demonstrates successful plant growth under specified red plus blue photon flux.
  8. Game-specific data: Space Sprout Sleuth, Campaign 1 Case 03 runtime. Values include combined PPFD 280 umol m-2 s-1 and surface-to-habitat transmission values of 92% blue, 88% green, 31% red, and 12% deep red; combined PPFD 280 umol m-2 s-1; aggregate transmission 68%; filter replaced 47 sols ago. Exact commit provenance is retained in metadata and the runtime audit.

Authoritative sources support general definitions and mechanisms. Exact values and the Mars diagnosis are game-specific. All displayed percentages are direct game measurements.

Technical fallback and rights record

Fallback evidence digest
  • 280 PPFD, adequate.
  • 12 m silica light pipe, surface collector filter, and supplemental white LEDs.
  • Transmission: blue 92%, green 88%, red 31%, deep red 12%.
  • New leaves pale; old leaves greener; roots healthy.
  • Added nutrients did not help.
Current filter record
  • Collector filter replaced 47 sols ago; part number not recorded.
  • Required model: FS-7 FULL SPECTRUM.
  • Incorrect model: BP-4 BLUE PASS, which passes blue/green while causing strong red/deep-red rejection.
  • Mars dust may lower total transmission but does not explain blue/green passing while red is selectively rejected.

Figure rights

All figures are curriculum-original vector constructions. No publisher or NASA figures are copied.

Data rights and precision

The current game transmission values are graphed at whole-number precision. No continuous spectral curve or unreported wavelength values are inferred.
