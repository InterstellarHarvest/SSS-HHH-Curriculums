References and source-status guidance

  1. Authoritative standard/definition: Illuminating Engineering Society. Photosynthetic photon flux density (PPFD): photons per unit area and time in the 400-700 nm band. Used only to define the quantity metric.
  2. Primary research: McCree, K. J. (1971/1972). The action spectrum, absorptance and quantum yield of photosynthesis in crop plants. Agricultural Meteorology 9:191-216. Measurements across 22 crop species showed wavelength-dependent photosynthetic response with broad blue and red maxima.
  3. Primary research: Hogewoning, S. W., et al. (2010). Blue light dose-responses of leaf photosynthesis, morphology, and chemical composition of cucumber. Journal of Experimental Botany 61:3107-3117. Demonstrates that equal irradiance with different blue:red composition can produce different plant function.
  4. Primary research: Heyes, D. J., Ruban, A. V., and Hunter, C. N. (2003). Protochlorophyllide oxidoreductase: dark reactions of a light-driven enzyme. Biochemistry 42:523-528. Establishes POR as a light-driven chlorophyll-biosynthesis enzyme.
  5. Primary research: Armstrong, G. A., Runge, S., Frick, G., Sperling, U., and Apel, K. (1995). Identification of NADPH:protochlorophyllide oxidoreductases A and B in Arabidopsis thaliana. Plant Physiology 108:1505-1517. Establishes light-dependent protochlorophyllide reduction in angiosperm chlorophyll biosynthesis.
  6. Primary/technical space-horticulture source: Bula, R. J., et al. (1994). Light emitting diodes as a plant lighting source. NASA Technical Reports Server. Controlled-environment lighting must provide adequate PAR flux and spectral regions needed for normal development.
  7. Primary/technical space-horticulture source: Bula, R. J., et al. (1991). Light-emitting diodes as a radiation source for plants. HortScience/NASA record. Demonstrates successful plant growth under specified red plus blue photon flux.
  8. Game-specific data: Space Sprout Sleuth, Campaign 1 Case 03 runtime. Values include combined PPFD 280 µmol/m²/s and surface-to-habitat transmission values of 92% blue, 88% green, 31% red, and 12% deep red; combined PPFD 280 µmol/m²/s; aggregate transmission 68%; filter replaced 47 sols ago. Exact commit provenance is retained in metadata and the runtime audit.

Answer Key

Completed exemplar - data analysis

REFERENCE1 · Define the measurement

PPFD counts photons across the defined waveband per area and time. It does not reveal how photons are distributed by wavelength.

DATA ANALYSIS2 · Read the spectral-transmission data

Blue 92%; green 88%; red 31%; deep red 12%. Red is the lowest-transmission band inside 400-700 nm.

DATA ANALYSIS3 · Compare quantity and quality

The 280 PPFD reading is explicitly adequate, so simple low total quantity conflicts with the sensor evidence. The spectrum is uneven: red and deep red transmit far less than blue or green.

PATTERN ANALYSIS4 · Connect the symptom pattern

Old leaves already contain chlorophyll. New tissue must synthesize it, so new-growth bleaching fits a failure in new chlorophyll formation. Healthy roots and failed nutrient additions weaken root/nutrient explanations.

Best overall pattern conclusion: adequate total photon quantity is reaching the habitat, but the wavelength distribution is selectively depleted where the case mechanism needs it for healthy new chlorophyll formation.
