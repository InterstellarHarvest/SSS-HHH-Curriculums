# Mars Habitat - Teacher Quick Start

## What students do
Play Case 03, graph four direct transmission readings, compare spectral distribution with adequate total PPFD, diagnose the wrong-filter failure, and write CER.

## Critical game values
280 umol m-2 s-1 PPFD; 12 m silica light pipe; 68% aggregate transmission; 47-sol filter replacement; blue 92%, green 88%, red 31%, deep red 12%; FS-7 required, BP-4 wrong model.

## Correct diagnosis
The light delivery system is filtering out red wavelengths needed for chlorophyll biosynthesis.

## Essential science
An integrated photon quantity does not reveal wavelength distribution. Two lighting systems can have similar PPFD while differing substantially in spectral composition.

## Likely sticking point
Students may say “the collector is dusty.” Dust can lower total transmission, but the game explicitly says it does not explain blue/green passing while red is rejected.
