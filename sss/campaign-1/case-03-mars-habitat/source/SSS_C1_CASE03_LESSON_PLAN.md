# Mars Habitat - Formal Lesson Plan

**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Student identity:** Data Analyst  
**Core science:** light spectrum versus total intensity  
**Status:** VALIDATION BUILD

## Overview
Students analyze a Mars potato-growth failure by distinguishing aggregate photon quantity from wavelength distribution. They graph the four current runtime transmission values, compare them with the adequate 280 PPFD reading, identify selective red/deep-red rejection at the collector, and explain the new-growth bleaching pattern.

## Objectives
Students will interpret PPFD as an integrated quantity; analyze exact wavelength-band transmission; reject simple low-total-light and dust-only explanations; connect the wrong filter to the pigment symptom; and write a concise CER.

## Success criteria
- Uses **280**, **92%**, **88%**, **31%**, and **12%** exactly.
- Identifies red at 31% as the lowest-transmission band within 400-700 nm.
- States that blue/green passing while red/deep red are rejected is a distribution problem, not proof of low total PPFD.
- Uses the **47-sol filter replacement**, missing part number, and BP-4/FS-7 record as engineering evidence.
- Avoids “more brightness always fixes it,” “plants only use red,” and “green light is useless.”

## Standards alignment
NGSS MS-LS1-6; NGSS MS-PS4-2; analyzing and interpreting data; constructing explanations; cause and effect; systems and system models.

## Materials and preparation
Game or fallback digest; Student/Accessible/Grayscale Mission; pencil; calculator optional but unnecessary. Open Case 03 and keep the correct diagnosis private.

## Timing
| Time | Phase | Action |

## Procedure
1. Ask what a single PPFD number can hide.
2. At **Task 2 - Read the spectral-transmission data**, require the four direct runtime values; no loss conversion or invented intermediate points.
3. At **Task 3 - Compare quantity and quality**, require explicit rejection of low total PPFD.
4. Prompt students to distinguish dust, which lowers total transmission, from the observed selective rejection pattern.
5. At **Task 6 - Model the mechanism**, require the wrong collector filter and the symptom location.
6. Collect the mission sheet or Tasks 2-9.

## Checks for understanding
Can the student explain why 280 PPFD does not describe wavelength distribution? Can the student identify red at 31% as the weakest in-PAR band? Can the student explain why the BP-4 record fits better than dust alone?

## Differentiation
Use the Accessible Mission, oral dictation, speech-to-text, direct-value table, and technical fallback without reducing reasoning demand.

## Misconceptions
PPFD is a complete spectrum; more brightness always repairs poor spectral distribution; dust explains selective rejection; only red matters; yellowing automatically proves nutrient deficiency.

## Assessment
Use the quick or formal rubric. Do not grade game score, speed, rapport, or clue-discovery order.
