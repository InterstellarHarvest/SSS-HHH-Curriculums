# Mars Habitat - Teacher Case Analysis

## Story problem
Potatoes grow normally for about three weeks; then new leaves become pale/white while older lower leaves retain green.

## Evidence channels
- Crew: timing, top-down progression, failed nutrient additions.
- Sensors: 280 PPFD; 12 m full-spectrum silica light pipe; 68% aggregate transmission; direct band transmissions 92/88/31/12.
- Plants: healthy roots and pigment failure concentrated in new tissue.
- Archive: FS-7 filter required; BP-4 blue-pass filter rejects most red/deep red; filter replaced 47 sols ago without logged part number.

## Essential evidence
Adequate 280 PPFD; blue/green high transmission versus red/deep-red low transmission; wrong-filter maintenance record; new-versus-old tissue pattern.

## Distractors
Perchlorates conflict with healthy roots and a prepared system. CO2 is within game tolerance. Mars-sol timing does not match the pigment pattern. Dust lowers total transmission but does not explain the selective spectral pattern.

## Mechanism
Within the game model, a wrong collector filter passes blue/green and rejects most red/deep red. Total PPFD remains adequate, but the case-critical red delivery is insufficient; the light-driven chlorophyll-development step in new tissue is impaired, so new growth bleaches first.

## Scientific caution
POR is light-driven, but do not teach that every plant process uses only red light. Primary research supports important blue and red responses, and green photons can contribute within leaves. The exact filter models and numbers are game-specific.
