# Mars Habitat - Quick and Formal Rubrics

## Quick rubric
| Dimension | Secure | Developing | Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data | Uses 280 and 92/88/31/12 exactly | Minor error | Omits or invents values |
| Quantity vs spectrum | Clear distinction | Partial | Conflates them |
| Pattern interpretation | Identifies selective red/deep-red rejection | Notices unevenness | Treats all bands alike |
| Mechanism | Connects wrong filter to new-growth bleaching | Cause only | No mechanism |
| Alternative rejection | Uses evidence against low total light/dust or another option | Weak | None |
| Communication | Complete, clear CER | Incomplete | Major gaps |

## Formal analytic rubric
Seven dimensions, 4 points each: data accuracy; graph interpretation; quantity/spectrum distinction; mechanism; alternative evaluation; source-status/uncertainty; communication/completion. Maximum 28. Game score, speed, and rapport are excluded.
