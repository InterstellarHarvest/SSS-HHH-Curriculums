# Mars Habitat - Completed Exemplar Answer Key

## Task 1 - Define the measurement
PPFD counts photons across the defined photosynthetic waveband per unit area and time. It does not show how those photons are distributed among wavelength bands.

## Task 2 - Read the spectral-transmission data
Blue 92%; green 88%; red 31%; deep red 12%. Red is the lowest-transmission band within 400-700 nm. Deep red is lower but lies beyond the stated 400-700 nm band.

## Task 3 - Compare quantity and quality
The game calls 280 PPFD adequate, so simple low total quantity conflicts with the sensor reading. The spectral distribution is strongly uneven: blue and green pass at 92% and 88%, while red and deep red pass at only 31% and 12%.

## Task 4 - Connect the symptom pattern
Older leaves already contain chlorophyll; new leaves must produce new pigment. Healthy roots and failed nutrient additions weaken root/nutrient explanations, while new-growth bleaching fits impaired new chlorophyll development.

## Task 5 - Select and reject diagnoses
Correct: The light delivery system is filtering out red wavelengths needed for chlorophyll biosynthesis. Reject dust-only/low-total-light: the game says dust lowers total transmission but cannot explain blue and green passing while red is selectively rejected; 280 PPFD is still adequate.

## Task 6 - Model the mechanism
Unlogged filter replacement 47 sols ago -> wrong BP-4 blue-pass filter at collector -> blue/green pass but red/deep red are strongly rejected -> case-critical red delivery is insufficient for normal new chlorophyll development -> new growth bleaches.

## Task 7 - Write the case conclusion
Claim: Selective spectrum filtering, not low total light, caused the crop failure. Evidence: 280 PPFD is adequate; blue/green transmission is 92/88%; red/deep-red transmission is only 31/12%; new tissue bleaches while older leaves retain green; the filter part number was not logged after replacement. Reasoning: PPFD integrates photon quantity and can hide an unbalanced spectrum. The maintenance and spectral pattern identify a filter that preserves blue/green while rejecting red wavelengths used by the game-defined chlorophyll mechanism, explaining why newly formed tissue lacks pigment.

## Task 8 - Transfer the analysis
Increasing brightness could raise PPFD while preserving the same distorted wavelength proportions. Inspect/replace the collector filter and verify output with a spectrometer.

## Task 9 - Exit ticket
Compare total PPFD with a wavelength-resolved spectral measurement.
