## 3 · Model the pollination sequence

**Task 3 word bank:** fruit set · physical agitation · viable pollen in anthers · fertilization · pollen tube growth · pollen reaches stigma

**Completed exemplar:**

1. **viable pollen in anthers**
2. **physical agitation**
3. **pollen reaches stigma**
4. **pollen tube growth**
5. **fertilization**
6. **fruit set**

**Model connection:** Each arrow means the earlier event makes the later event possible. A failure does not guarantee that every later structure is damaged; it means the later event cannot proceed normally in this case.

## 4 · Identify the failed step

- **Failed step:** Step 2 — **physical agitation** that releases pollen from the anthers.
- **Observation 1:** Plant examination shows abundant, apparently viable pollen sitting undisturbed on the anthers while the stigmas remain clean.
- **Observation 2:** Sensor and design records show nearly still air, no insects, and no installed manual or mechanical pollination system.
- **Downstream result:** Pollen does not reach the stigma, so pollen-tube growth, fertilization, and fruit set do not occur; flowers eventually drop.

Reasonable wording may say “pollen release/transfer” as long as the response locates the first failure before stigma contact.

## 6 · Diagnose and reject an alternative

**Diagnosis exemplar:** The tomato crop is failing at the pollen-release and transfer stage. The sealed greenhouse supplies no effective flower agitation, so pollen remains on the anthers instead of reaching the stigma. That stops the later steps needed for fruit set.

**Rejected-alternative exemplar:** Lunar-regolith toxicity is tempting because the plants grow in a regolith-compost blend, but the roots, leaves, stems, moisture, and nutrients are healthy. It also does not explain why pollen remains undisturbed on normal flowers.

## 7 · Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

**Claim:** The tomatoes do not set fruit because the greenhouse omitted an effective pollination process, especially the physical agitation needed to release pollen and move it to the stigma.

**Evidence:** Plant examination shows mature pollen still on the anthers and clean stigmas. The sensor array reports nearly still air and no fans. Design records list the pollination strategy as unresolved, and the greenhouse contains no insects or manual system.

**Reasoning:** Healthy flowers can produce viable pollen without completing reproduction. In tomato flowers, pollen must be released and reach a receptive stigma before it can germinate and grow a pollen tube. Because the case lacks agitation and the pollen remains where it formed, the sequence stops before stigma contact; fertilization and fruit set therefore cannot follow.

## 8 · Design a reliable pollination support

**Design exemplar:** Install a scheduled, targeted flower-cluster vibration system that gently agitates each cluster while flowers are open. Include a manual backup procedure for equipment outages.

**Criterion:** The system must consistently release pollen and produce visible evidence of stigma contact/early fruit set across open flower clusters.

**Constraint:** It must operate in a sealed habitat with limited power, mass, crew time, and no animal pollinators, without damaging flowers.

**Mechanism check:** The system restores Step 2, **physical agitation**. The crew should verify that pollen is released during operation and then monitor for stigma contact and early ovary swelling rather than assuming a running device equals biological success.

Other defensible designs include a verified manual cluster-agitation schedule or carefully controlled airflow, provided the response explains the restored step, avoids flower damage, and includes a measurable success check.

## 9 · Exit ticket

**Completed exemplar:** I would first test the pollen-release and stigma-contact steps, because the new habitat has already confirmed flowers and viable pollen. I would inspect whether pollen remains trapped on the anthers and whether receptive stigmas receive grains before and after controlled flower agitation. If agitation releases pollen and later fruit begins to set, the failed step was physical release/transfer. If pollen already reaches the stigma, investigators should test the later steps such as germination, pollen-tube growth, or fertilization instead.
