## Mission question

Why do healthy lunar tomato plants form flowers but no fruit?

## Science focus — Pollination as a process

Tomato flowers can contain both pollen-producing and pollen-receiving structures, but successful fruit production still depends on a sequence of events. Pollen must be released, reach a receptive stigma, germinate, grow a pollen tube, and complete fertilization before the ovary can begin normal fruit development.

**Science status:** The tomato reproductive sequence is established science. The Shackleton Crater greenhouse, exact sensor readings, and design history are fictional case evidence.
