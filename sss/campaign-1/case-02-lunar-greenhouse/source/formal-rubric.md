# Formal Analytic Rubric — Lunar Greenhouse

**Status:** VALIDATION BUILD

| Dimension | 4 — Advanced | 3 — Proficient | 2 — Developing | 1 — Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Process-model accuracy | Sequences all events accurately, distinguishes pollination from fertilization, and shows downstream dependency. | Sequences the required events accurately with only minor wording differences. | Includes most events but merges or misorders one important step. | Model is substantially incomplete or scientifically incorrect. |
| Failed-step diagnosis | Locates the first failure precisely and explains why later events stop. | Identifies missing physical agitation/pollen release as the first failure. | Identifies general pollination failure but not the first step. | Selects an unsupported failure or gives no mechanism. |
| Evidence selection | Uses precise evidence from three or more relevant sources and differentiates symptom, condition, and design record. | Uses at least two specific pieces from more than one source. | Uses one strong piece or multiple vague pieces. | Evidence is irrelevant, copied without purpose, or absent. |
| Alternative evaluation | Rejects a plausible alternative with direct contradictory evidence and explains why it fails mechanistically. | Rejects one alternative using relevant case evidence. | Names an alternative but gives weak or incomplete rejection. | Does not evaluate an alternative. |
| CER reasoning | Builds a concise, coherent causal explanation from evidence through mechanism to fruit-set failure. | Connects claim, evidence, and mechanism accurately. | Claim and evidence are present but the causal connection is incomplete. | Evidence is listed without reasoning or the claim is unsupported. |
| Engineering response | Provides a mechanism-matched design with criterion, constraint, verification, and a meaningful trade-off. | Provides design, criterion, constraint, and mechanism check. | Provides a plausible design but omits one required component or verification. | Provides a generic fix that does not restore the failed step. |
| Scientific language and qualification | Uses vocabulary precisely and explicitly limits claims to tomato/context where appropriate. | Uses key vocabulary accurately with no serious overgeneralization. | Some vocabulary confusion or overgeneralization is present. | Major terminology errors obscure understanding. |
| Communication and completion | All required fields are complete, readable, and appropriately concise. | Required work is substantially complete and understandable. | Several gaps reduce clarity. | Major sections are missing. |

The formal rubric may be converted to local numeric scoring by a teacher, but gameplay performance remains excluded.
