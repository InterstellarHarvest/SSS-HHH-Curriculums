# Teacher Quick Start — Lunar Greenhouse

**Status:** VALIDATION BUILD

## Purpose

Students act as **Process Modelers**. They reconstruct the tomato pollination-to-fruit-set sequence, locate the first failed step, reject distractors, and propose a reliable support method for a sealed habitat.

## Materials

- One Student or Accessible mission packet per student
- Internet-capable device and headphones when available
- *Space Sprout Sleuth*, Campaign 1, Case 02 — Lunar Greenhouse
- Optional technical-fallback evidence digest

## 60-minute route

| Time | Action |
|---:|---|
| 0–5 min | Open the game and packet. |
| 5–10 min | Launch the flower-without-fruit phenomenon; students complete **2 · Initial thinking**. |
| 10–28 min | Students gather one formal clue from Crew, Sensors, Plants, and Logs. |
| 28–38 min | Students complete **3 · Model the pollination sequence** and **4 · Identify the failed step**. |
| 38–48 min | Students submit the game diagnosis and complete **5 · Test the competing explanations**, **6 · Diagnose and reject an alternative**, and **7 · Claim-Evidence-Reasoning**. |
| 48–55 min | Students complete **8 · Design a reliable pollination support**. |
| 55–60 min | Students complete **9 · Exit ticket** independently. |

## Correct diagnosis

Pollination fails because the sealed greenhouse provides almost no flower agitation: no insects, no meaningful air movement, and no implemented manual or mechanical pollination plan. Mature pollen remains on the anthers instead of being released and reaching the stigma.

## Essential science

“Self-pollinating” does not mean “no pollen movement required.” In tomato flowers, physical agitation can release pollen; pollination must be followed by pollen germination, pollen-tube growth, fertilization, and fruit set.

## Likely sticking point

Students may jump from “no bees” directly to the answer. Require them to locate the failure in the sequence and use the undisturbed-pollen observation. Also prevent the overgeneralization that every flowering crop uses the same pollination mechanism.

## Collect

Collect the completed mission packet. Do not use game score, speed, rapport, or optional-node completion as academic evidence.

## Technical fallback

Provide `technical-notes.md` evidence digest. Students can complete the same numbered tasks without gameplay. Technical failure must not become academic failure.
