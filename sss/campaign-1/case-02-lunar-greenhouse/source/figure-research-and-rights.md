# Figure Research and Rights Decision — Pollination Process Model

**Status:** VALIDATION BUILD

## Research reviewed

- University of Alaska Fairbanks, *Pollination & Fruit Development in Tomatoes*, Figure 1 and accompanying process description.
- UC Davis Tomato Genetics Resource Center, *Guidelines for Emasculating and Pollinating Tomato Flowers*, technique photographs and sequence.
- University of Connecticut, *Pollination of Greenhouse Tomatoes*, mechanism and practice description.

## Rights disposition

The reviewed pages were used for factual research only. No reviewed image carried a clear open license suitable for adaptation into this repository. No external figure was copied, traced, recolored, or embedded.

## Production decision

Create a curriculum-original, editable SVG process schematic. The figure:

- uses six orthogonally connected stages;
- marks the failed step with hatch, outline, text, and position rather than color alone;
- remains legible in grayscale and ordinary photocopies;
- includes a complete accessible title and description;
- uses real vector text;
- appears completed in the Answer Key and blank/interactive in Student and Accessible editions;
- is attributed as curriculum-original in its caption metadata band.
