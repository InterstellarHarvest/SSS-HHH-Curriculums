## Diagram research and rights decision

The production audit reviewed the University of Alaska Fairbanks tomato-flower schematic and the UC Davis pollination-technique images to understand common labeling and process order. Neither source page provides an explicit open-content license authorizing adaptation into this curriculum package. The sources are therefore cited for factual research but **no figure was copied, traced, or adapted**.

The included `assets/pollination-process-model.svg` is curriculum-original. Its geometry, labels, arrow structure, failure marker, grayscale patterns, and accessible description were created specifically for this package. It depicts a process sequence, not a botanical illustration, and remains editable vector text.
