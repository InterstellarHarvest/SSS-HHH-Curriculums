# Quick Rubric — Lunar Greenhouse

**Status:** VALIDATION BUILD

| Dimension | Secure | Developing | Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Uses specific case observations, including undisturbed pollen and at least one independent source. | Uses some relevant evidence but misses the bottleneck or source diversity. | Uses vague clues, copied text without interpretation, or irrelevant evidence. |
| Mechanism | Correctly sequences pollination, pollen-tube growth, fertilization, and fruit set; locates the first failure at pollen release/agitation. | Identifies pollination failure but collapses steps or leaves the causal chain incomplete. | Names “no bees” or another cause without a working biological sequence. |
| Reasoning | Connects evidence to the failed step and rejects an alternative. | Gives a partial connection or weak alternative rejection. | Lists evidence without explaining why it supports the claim. |
| Communication / completion | Uses key vocabulary accurately and completes Tasks 3–9, including criterion and constraint. | Minor vocabulary or completion gaps. | Major gaps make the model or diagnosis difficult to evaluate. |

No numeric points appear on the Student packet by default.
