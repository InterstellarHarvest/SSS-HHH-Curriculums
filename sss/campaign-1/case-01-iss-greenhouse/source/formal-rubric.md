# Formal Analytic Rubric — ISS Greenhouse Module

Use for portfolio, research, or graduate-program documentation.

| Criterion | 4 — Accomplished | 3 — Proficient | 2 — Developing | 1 — Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence selection | Selects the most relevant evidence and distinguishes symptom from cause. | Selects relevant evidence with minor omissions. | Includes mixed relevant and irrelevant evidence. | Provides little usable evidence. |
| Source diversity | Integrates three or four source types. | Uses at least two source types. | Relies mostly on one source. | Does not identify sources clearly. |
| Mechanistic explanation | Accurately explains microgravity, statolith direction, and gravitropic response with appropriate qualification. | Explains the central mechanism accurately. | Names the mechanism but leaves important gaps. | Gives an unsupported or inaccurate mechanism. |
| Alternative evaluation | Rejects a plausible alternative using specific contradictory evidence. | Rejects an alternative with generally relevant evidence. | Rejects an alternative with weak support. | Does not evaluate an alternative. |
| Engineering reasoning | Defines a feasible cue, criterion, and constraint tied to the problem. | Provides a relevant solution with a criterion or constraint. | Provides a loosely related solution. | Provides a solution that does not address orientation. |
| Communication | Is clear, concise, complete, and uses vocabulary accurately. | Is mostly clear and complete. | Is understandable but inconsistent. | Is incomplete or difficult to interpret. |

No numeric points appear on the Student worksheet by default.
