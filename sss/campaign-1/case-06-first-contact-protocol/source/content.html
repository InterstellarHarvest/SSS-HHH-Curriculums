Mission question

What disrupted the three-organism partnership after docking, and how can the crews respond without weakening life-support safety?

Case briefing

The Zhel'ora docked at Hayes Station about three days ago. Its canopy, roots, and network once functioned as a coordinated partnership. The network is now dormant, while canopy and root activity is reduced and uncoordinated.

Reasoning rule

An observation reports what was detected or recorded. An interpretation explains what observations may mean. Timing can support a cause, but correlation alone does not prove it.

Teacher Guide

Lesson overview

Students investigate why a fictional three-organism food partnership lost coordination after docking. They classify evidence, build a systems-interruption model, reject three distractors, compare interventions, write CER, and transfer the model.

Learning target

I can model how a change to one part of a system can interrupt chemical signaling and partnership function, then use evidence to recommend a constrained response.

Correct diagnosis

Human atmospheric processing removed volatile Zhel'ii signal compounds; failed signal persistence triggered fictional network dormancy and loss of coordination.

Duration

One 60-minute lesson. Gameplay target: 13–22 minutes depending on optional dialogue.

Collect

Student pages 1–5. Score all eight tasks; independently score Tasks 5, 7, and 8.

Materials and preparation

  • Case 06 game access or the verified evidence table on Student page 2
  • Student Mission or Accessible Mission; writing tools
  • Teacher Guide and Answer Key
  • Preview the four required source routes and confirm the frozen Case 06 runtime is available

Teacher Guide · Standards and success
SolarAgriculturalAgency

Measurable objectives

By the end of the lesson, students will:

  1. classify observations and interpretations with at least 3 of 3 correct;
  2. construct a four-part systems model with scientifically coherent links;
  3. reject three distractors using specific conflicting evidence;
  4. recommend a selective response that states a safety constraint and monitoring evidence; and
  5. write a CER using at least two independent case sources.

Standards alignment and limitation

StatusNGSS dimension or performance expectationWhere demonstrated
AssessedSEP: Developing and Using ModelsTask 4 · Model the Broken Coordination System requires a linked systems-interruption model.
AssessedCCC: Systems and System ModelsTasks 4, 6, and 8 reason across boundaries, interactions, constraints, and feedback.
SupportingMS-LS2-4 component work; DCI LS2.CStudents predict an effect of disrupting a biological relationship. Alien evidence cannot serve as empirical proof about Earth ecosystems, so the full PE is not claimed.
SupportingMS-ETS1-2 component work; DCI ETS1.BTask 6 · Recommend a Monitored Signal-Safe Response compares design responses using criteria, constraints, and evidence. It does not complete a full engineering design cycle.

Success criteria

  • Model includes atmospheric processing → lower signal persistence → dormancy response → failed coordination.
  • Diagnosis uses evidence from the interface, organism inspection, archive, and timing without treating timing as proof.
  • Intervention is selective, monitored, reversible, and compatible with pressure, breathable-gas, and contaminant controls.
  • Transfer response preserves the mechanism while treating the new example as a testable prediction.

Timing and causation

Docking occurred 72.4 hours ago. The last signal occurred 72.1 hours ago—0.3 hours, or 18 minutes, after docking. Because a larger “hours ago” value is earlier, this supports the sequence “docking, then signal loss.” It remains correlation, not proof. Causal support comes from converging filtration, missing-signal, organism-state, and archive evidence.

Science and fiction distinctions

ClassificationUse in instruction
Established Earth scienceOrganisms can use chemical cues; symbioses involve close biological relationships; trace-contaminant systems may use adsorption and catalytic oxidation.
Debated or context-dependent Earth interpretationEffects attributed to common mycorrhizal networks vary by organism, setting, pathway, and study design. Avoid “mother tree,” forest-wide sharing, plant hearing, internet, and superorganism claims.
Plausible engineering extrapolationA monitored, isolated, or selectively configured treatment zone can be evaluated while life-support safeguards remain active.
Fictional alien biology and measurementThe three-organism network, exact compounds, counts, efficiencies, thresholds, dormancy response, and recovery behavior are game evidence.
Curriculum-created modelThe four-stage diagram organizes audited evidence; it is an explanatory representation, not another observation.

Teacher Guide · Launch and evidence
SolarAgriculturalAgency

Procedures: Tasks 1–3

0–6 min
Task 1 · Predict What Changed at Docking

Ask students to name a changed variable, not a final diagnosis. Accept light, temperature, pressure, filtration, or physical connection as testable starting predictions. Require a matching observation.

6–10 min
Task 2 · Separate Observation from Interpretation

Have students classify independently before discussion. Ask, “What additional observation would make the interpretation stronger?”

10–24 min
Task 3 · Connect the Four Evidence Sources

During gameplay, direct students only to the four source categories. If gameplay is unavailable, the evidence table is a complete fallback. Do not supply the diagnosis before students record each contribution.

Discussion guidance

  • What remained nominal, and what changed?
  • Which evidence describes the atmosphere? Which describes biological response?
  • Why does “still alive” not mean “functioning as a coordinated system”?
  • What is the difference between a signal being produced, persisting, and being received?
  • How does the 18-minute order narrow hypotheses without proving one?

Likely misconceptions and responses

MisconceptionTeacher response
“Clean air is always biologically better.”Ask what “clean” means to a human-standard database and whether an unfamiliar but necessary molecule could be removed.
“The signal stopped exactly at docking, so docking proves the cause.”Reconstruct 72.4 → 72.1 hours ago and require other evidence.
“Dormant means dead.”Point to reduced canopy/root activity and the archive’s reversible response.
“Nominal atmosphere means nothing changed.”Distinguish primary conditions from trace compounds that were not routinely monitored.

Teacher Guide · Model and diagnose
SolarAgriculturalAgency

Procedures: Tasks 4–6

24–32 min
Task 4 · Model the Broken Coordination System

Require a change in every box and causal language on every arrow. Ask students to identify the system boundary: shared docking atmosphere plus the three-organism partnership.

32–39 min
Task 5 · Compare and Reject Competing Diagnoses

Students must use conflicting evidence to reject all three alternatives, not simply circle the correct choice. Press for convergence rather than one decisive clue.

39–48 min
Task 6 · Recommend a Monitored Signal-Safe Response

Students compare unsafe shutdown, no change, and selective treatment. Require criteria, constraints, and monitoring. The correct response is a testable class of intervention, not permission to implement an unverified filter exemption.

Intervention caution

Do not accept blanket disablement. Human atmospheric processing protects pressure, breathable gas, and contaminant control. A defensible recommendation isolates the shared atmosphere or reprograms docking-section treatment for identified signals while maintaining monitoring, reversibility, and life-support safeguards.

Engineering comparison prompts

  • Need: restore signal persistence and coordination without adding crew risk.
  • Criteria: identified signal compounds persist; network activity and nutrient transfer resume; crew atmosphere remains safe.
  • Constraints: unknown alien chemistry, incomplete hazard data, shared atmosphere, pressure control, contaminant protection, and need for reversibility.
  • Verification: compare inlet/outlet compound profiles, network signal activity, canopy/root coordination, and crew life-support readings over time.

Language and representation cautions

  • Use “chemical signal” or “chemical cue,” not hearing, talking, language, internet, or intentional conversation as Earth-science claims.

Case boundary

Case 06 ends with a supported diagnosis and a cautious proposed response. Do not introduce germination-pod evidence, the named germination sequence, or the resolution of The Gift. Case 07 remains a separate investigation and the Campaign 1 culmination.

Activated-carbon dependence and monitoring

Answer Key · CER exemplar
SolarAgriculturalAgency

CLAIM

Human atmospheric processing is the best-supported cause of the network dormancy: it removed the volatile compounds needed for the fictional partnership’s coordination.

EVIDENCE

The interface reports human filtration engaged and a trace-compound decline from 847 to 12, with detected network signaling compounds at zero. Inspection shows inert but not visibly damaged signal structures and active but uncoordinated canopy and roots. The archive states that failed signal persistence triggers dormancy. Docking at 72.4 hours ago precedes the last signal at 72.1 hours ago by 0.3 hours.

REASONING

Filtration provides a mechanism for the missing compounds. When signals cannot persist, the fictional network’s feedback response reduces activity. Without the network, root and canopy products are not requested or transferred, so partnership function declines even though the organisms remain alive. Timing supports this sequence but does not prove it alone; the converging atmosphere, organism, and archive evidence makes filtration the strongest diagnosis and weakens damage, journey drift, and incompatibility.

Reasonable variations

Accept “scrubbers,” “human-side filtration,” or “docking-section treatment” if the response identifies removal of signaling compounds, not simply docking. Evidence may be worded differently but must come from more than one source and preserve the correlation-versus-causation qualification.

Answer Key · Transfer and exit
SolarAgriculturalAgency

Prediction and mechanism: If molecule M is the coordinating signal and a selective test allows it to persist, coordination between the engineered partners should increase. This follows the model: treatment change → signal persistence → receiver response → partnership function.

Safety control: Use a sealed comparison chamber while pressure, breathable gases, contaminants, and microbial containment remain monitored. Include an unchanged-filter control chamber.

Evidence to collect: Measure M upstream and downstream, record both partners’ coordinated outputs, and compare with the unchanged-filter control. Persistence without restored coordination would weaken the prediction; restored coordination only in the selective chamber would support it.

Independent exit response: Human atmospheric processing is the best-supported diagnosis because it engaged at docking, removed detected network signals, and matches the archive’s dormancy mechanism. Docking preceded the last signal by 18 minutes, but timing alone cannot rule out another docking-related cause; the missing-compound and organism-state evidence is also necessary.
