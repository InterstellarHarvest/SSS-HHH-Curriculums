## C2C4-ACC02 — Task 8 grading contract differs across editions

Student Task 8 asks for dark hours per cycle and specifically asks the learner to justify the chosen value **rather than the five-hour trial minimum**. Teacher guidance likewise cautions against turning the minimum directly into the specification, and the Answer Key specifies at least 6.0 h because the 18/6 schedule has a two-year successful record.

Accessible Task 8 instead says:

- trials found at least 5 dark hours maintained signalling
- the successful two-year schedule used 6 dark hours
- **“You must choose one and say why.”**

That wording makes a justified 5-hour specification appear acceptable, but the common key would grade against a 6-hour expectation.

### Required remediation

Align the Accessible prompt with the shared assessment contract. Recommended wording: require a specification with margin above the recorded minimum and explicitly direct the learner to explain why the six-hour historically successful schedule is safer evidence than specifying the minimum itself.

Do not silently grade a learner response as wrong when the Accessible sheet explicitly invited it.
