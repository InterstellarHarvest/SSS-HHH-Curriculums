### Accessible differentiation

### Fillable response
C1C3–C1C6 confirm a systemic required-selection defect: printed circle/choice operations can lack persisted digital controls.

### Implementation leakage
C1C2/C1C4 print runtime clue IDs; C1C5 prints internal node paths. Teacher-facing content needs a durable visible-label-only contract.

### Science-status discipline
C1C5–C1C6 provide strong models for separating observed evidence, qualified inference, scenario-specific claims, and broader science context.

### Identity
