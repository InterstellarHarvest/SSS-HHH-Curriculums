## C2C2-PACE01 — 110-minute route

## C2C2-TASK01 — `OK / ?` mixes two different judgments

Task 1 says write `OK` if the record settles a condition and the student can stop investigating it, or `?` if it deserves a second look.

The key marks:

- normal environment, viable pollen, receptive stigma = `OK`
- no pollinators, zero airflow, no periodic vibration = `?`

The problem is conceptual, not scientific: **pollinator absence and zero airflow are themselves settled observations.** They receive `?` because they remain diagnostically relevant, not because the readings are uncertain.

### Required remediation

Rename/restructure the code so students distinguish:

1. whether the observation itself is established, and
2. whether that observation rules out a cause or remains a useful lead.

A clearer single-column version could be `RULE OUT / KEEP INVESTIGATING`, or two separate judgments could be used if space allows.
